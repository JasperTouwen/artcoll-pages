artColl Support

artColl helps you catalogue an art collection — photos, artist and artwork details, and RFID/EPC tags with scannable barcodes.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email jasper.touwen@jasperjohns.nl. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Frequently asked

Where is my data stored? On your device, and in your private iCloud account if you’re signed in to iCloud. See our Privacy Policy.

My artworks don’t sync between devices. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive enabled, and connected to the internet.

How do the tags work? Each artwork gets a GIAI-96 EPC shown as a Code 128 or QR barcode. Scan it with your reader to find the piece, or write the EPC to a tag using your reader’s software.
