Privacy Policy for artColl

Last updated: 5 October 2026

artColl (“the app”) is an app for cataloguing a private art collection. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. Please read it together with Apple’s Privacy Policy, as the app relies on Apple’s iCloud service.

Summary

artColl does not collect, transmit to us, sell, or share any personal data. We (the developer) have no servers and no access to your catalogue. Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you enable iCloud, in your own private iCloud account.

What data the app stores

  • Artwork catalogue — titles, artists, medium, dimensions, edition, provenance, location, acquisition details, valuation, notes, photos you add, and the RFID/EPC tag identifiers the app generates.
  • Photos — images you choose from your photo library are stored inside the app’s own data (and your iCloud, if enabled). The app only accesses photos you explicitly select.

Where your data lives

Data is stored locally on your device using Apple’s Core Data. If you are signed in to iCloud, the data is synced to your own private iCloud (CloudKit) database so it can appear on your other devices. This storage is controlled by your Apple ID and governed by Apple’s terms. The developer cannot see or retrieve this data.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No account creation or login beyond your device’s own iCloud.
  • No data is sent to the developer or any third party.

Data deletion

You can delete any artwork, artist, or photo in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its local data; iCloud-synced data can be removed from Settings → your name → iCloud on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
